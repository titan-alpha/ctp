## Bidding & Optimization Strategy

### Bidding Setup

**Start with**: Lowest Cost (bid cap NOT recommended for small budgets)
- Why: $50 needs to spend efficiently without artificial limits
- Algorithm optimizes delivery automatically
- Best for conversion volume with small budgets

**If CPC exceeds $2.00 after 3 days**:
- Review targeting (may be too narrow)
- Check ad relevance score (should be "Above Average")
- Consider pausing and revising creative

### Budget Pacing

**Day 1-2 (Learning Phase)**:
- Instagram's algorithm is learning
- Don't make changes
- Expect uneven spending (may spend $15 first day, $3 second)
- Target: 3,000-5,000 impressions minimum

**Day 3-5 (Optimization)**:
- Review performance metrics
- CPC should be $1.00-$2.00
- CTR should be >1.5% (Reels standard)
- If CTR <1.0%: Creative issue (test new variation)
- If CPC >$2.50: Targeting too narrow

**Day 6-7 (Final Push)**:
- Let it run without changes
- Document all learnings
- Prepare scaling strategy if successful

### Dayparting Strategy

**Best Hours** (for productivity tools):
- 7AM-9AM EST (morning routine, commute)
- 12PM-2PM EST (lunch break scrolling)
- 6PM-9PM EST (evening wind-down)

**Instagram Note**: Platform auto-optimizes delivery time based on when YOUR audience is active. Manual dayparting not available for small budgets.

**Best Days**:
- Tuesday-Thursday (highest engagement)
- Sunday evening (planning for week ahead)
- Avoid: Friday night, Saturday (low productivity mindset)

- ❌ Relevance score "Below Average"

- ⚠️ CPC between $1.50-$2.50 (widen targeting slightly)
- ⚠️ CTR between 0.4-0.75% (test new copy)
- ⚠️ High clicks, zero tool usage (landing page issue)
- ⚠️ Video views but no clicks (add stronger CTA)

## Instagram-Specific Best Practices

### Creative Optimization

**What Works on Instagram Reels Ads**:
- ✅ First 3 seconds hook (your warm peach background does this)
- ✅ Vertical video optimized for mobile
- ✅ Minimal text overlay (Instagram auto-adds captions)
- ✅ Native feel (doesn't scream "ad")
- ✅ Under 15 seconds (9.8s is perfect)

**What Hurts Performance**:
- ❌ Horizontal video (black bars on mobile)
- ❌ Heavy branding in first 3 seconds
- ❌ Audio-dependent messaging (many watch muted)
- ❌ Overly polished/corporate aesthetic
- ❌ Long videos (>30 seconds)

### Account Setup Requirements

**Before launching campaign**:
1. Instagram Business Account (convert from personal)
2. Facebook Page linked to Instagram
3. Meta Business Manager account
4. Meta Pixel installed on ConveniencePro.cc
5. Payment method added to Ads Manager
6. Ad account verified (may require ID upload)

**Meta Pixel Events to Track**:
```javascript
// Page view
fbq('track', 'PageView');

// Visited tool page
fbq('track', 'ViewContent', {
  content_name: 'PDF Merger',
  content_category: 'PDF Tools'
});

// Used any tool
fbq('track', 'Lead', {
  content_name: 'Tool Used',
  value: 1.00,
  currency: 'USD'
});

// Bookmark/return visit
fbq('track', 'AddToWishlist');
```

## Instagram vs Reddit: Which to Test First?

### Test Instagram First IF:

- ✅ You have video creative ready (you do!)
- ✅ Your product is visual or easy to demonstrate
- ✅ You want faster feedback (3-5 days vs 7-10)
- ✅ You prefer platform-managed targeting
- ✅ Mobile traffic is priority

### Test Reddit First IF:

- ✅ Your audience is highly niche (developers, privacy advocates)
- ✅ You want lower CPMs ($0.50-$3 vs $4-$6)
- ✅ You value intent over impressions
- ✅ You can create platform-native image ads
- ✅ Desktop traffic is acceptable

### Recommendation: Test BOTH Simultaneously

**Why run both at same time**:
- Different audiences (Instagram: broader; Reddit: niche)
- Different intent (Instagram: discovery; Reddit: problem-solving)
- Faster learning (2 data points in same timeframe)
- Total investment still low ($100 total)
- Can double-down on winner in week 2

**How to allocate**:
- Instagram: $50 (Reels, 7 days)
- Reddit: $50 (Promoted Posts, 7 days)
- Compare results after week 1
- Scale winner by 4x in week 2
- Pause loser or reduce to $25/week
